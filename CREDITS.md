## Fonts
The menus and HUD are set in Barlow and Barlow Condensed (Medium, SemiBold, Bold, ExtraBold Italic) by Jeremy Tribby, from [Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts) ([the Barlow project](https://github.com/jpt/barlow)). Copyright 2017 The Barlow Project Authors, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which comes with the fonts in `Assets/_Project/Resources/Fonts/Barlow - OFL.txt`. TextMeshPro's Liberation Sans (SIL Open Font License, in `Assets/TextMesh Pro/Fonts`) comes with Unity and is only a fallback for characters Barlow lacks.

## Skies and surfaces
The skies in `Assets/_Project/Skies` are photographs of the whole sky from Poly Haven, and the surfaces' textures in `Assets/_Project/Surfaces` come from ambientCG. All are CC0 (no attribution required, credited anyway). The skies had their sun taken out (the game draws its own), the ground below their horizon replaced and their brightness scaled. The textures were cut down to 1024x1024 and only their color, bumps, roughness, ambient occlusion and height kept.

The umpire's and the line judges' voices are made with Windows' built-in text-to-speech (Microsoft Zira and Microsoft David), with a light public-address effect and the stadium's echo added (the tiebreak scores, the names alone and "Break point", "Match point" and "Tiebreak" were added the same way). The calls with your name are made the same way on your own computer while you play (on a Mac, with the Mac's own voice). Microsoft's terms for its voices apply; check them before selling the game.
